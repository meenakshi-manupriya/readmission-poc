# Explanations: Data Engineering Phase


## E1. config.py
Single source of truth for paths (computed relative to the code), the random seed (42), and the death/hospice exclusion codes.

## E2.ingestion.py
load_raw_data()
Fetches the dataset via ucimlrepo, falling back to a local CSV. Returns raw, untouched data.

## E3.validation.py
validate_raw_data()
Checks shape, key columns, and unique encounter_id. Raises an error if anything is off, so a pipeline can’t proceed on bad data.

## E4.cleaning.py
clean_data()
Runs five steps in order: build target Y, exclude death/hospice, exclude invalid gender, fix the "None"→NaN loading bug, drop weight/citoglipton/examide.

## E5.splitting.py
split_by_patient()
GroupShuffleSplit on patient_nbr, so all of a patient’s encounters land on one side.

## E6.demographics.py
build_demographic_features()
9 columns: race and gender one-hot, age band converted to its numeric midpoint.

## E7.encounter.py
build_encounter_features()
33 columns: true counts kept numeric, admission_type_id/admission_source_id one-hot (they’re codes, not magnitudes).

## E8.diagnoses.py
build_diagnosis_features()
30 columns: ~700 ICD-9 codes per slot grouped into 9 clinical categories plus Missing, for each of diag_1/2/3.

## E9.medications.py
build_medication_features()
36 columns: full status for 8 commonly used drugs, plus drug-count summaries covering all 21 drugs and two flags.

## E10.payer.py
build_payer_features()
11 columns: 9 common payer codes plus Other and Missing. Separate so it can be switched off for the with/without comparison.

## E11.build_features.py
build_features()
Orchestrator. Builds all modules, checks rows align, enforces train’s column schema on test, and blocks forbidden columns, duplicates and NaNs.

## E12.versioning.py
save_dataset_version() / load_dataset_version()
Saves parquet files plus manifest.json into v_<hash>/. The loader re-verifies content hashes.

## E13.pipeline.py
run_pipeline()
The single entry point that runs everything above.
